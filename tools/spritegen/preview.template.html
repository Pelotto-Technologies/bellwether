What ships

  • Per role: a sheet with 12 frames (walk ×4, idle ×2, wave ×2, talk ×2, raise, blink) plus a status-light mask row and a cloak mask row.
  • Strip ground, map tileset (grass, flowers, trees, rocks, 16 path and 16 water autotiles with a 2-frame shimmer).
  • Props: settlement, camp, 4-frame campfire, signpost, mount with walk cycle, tintable banner, glow and minimap pins.
  • UI: speech bubble and tail, nameplate, minimap frame (all 9-slice), and five status icons.
